Anatomy & scroll
  • Header and footer are fixed; only the body scrolls.
  • Header divider appears once body scrollTop > 0; footer shadow shows while content remains below.
  • Page behind is scroll-locked; no nested scroll areas in the body.
  • Drawer 400px (max 90vw); modal 440–640px, max-height 85vh; bottom sheet max 90% with footer riding above the soft keyboard.
Focus trap
  • role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby → title.
  • On open, focus the first field; for destructive or read-only dialogs, focus the safe button (Cancel).
  • Tab / Shift+Tab cycle inside; everything behind gets inert.
  • On close, return focus to the trigger (or its row if the trigger was removed).
Dismissal & motion
  • Esc, the close button, a scrim tap and swipe-down (sheet) all dismiss; with unsaved changes they ask “Discard changes?” first.
  • Grab handle is decorative; the close button is always there.
  • Record preview drawers can be non-modal (no scrim, no trap).
  • 240ms slide; reduced motion → opacity fade only.
