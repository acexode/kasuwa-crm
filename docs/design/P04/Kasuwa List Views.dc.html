Leads, Contacts, Accounts

One list pattern for all three objects, at 1440px and 360px. Everything works: search (names or numbers in any format), filter chips with live counts, saved views, the four view types, bulk actions, inline edit of Owner and Status, the column chooser, 25/50/100 paging, and swipe actions and the filter sheet on mobile.

Numbers firstPhones are stored and shown in E.164 (+234 803 123 4567) with a network chip. DND and WhatsApp opt-in sit next to the number.
Consent-aware bulkWhatsApp templates skip anyone without opt-in and say how many were skipped. Campaigns route DND numbers to WhatsApp or email only.
Programme scopeBPO agents see the programme chip and a note that only its records are visible. Locked columns show a lock, not an empty cell, and are left out of exports.
OfflineThe cached list stays usable with its last sync time. Edits queue with a cloud icon. Anything that needs a connection is disabled and says why.
