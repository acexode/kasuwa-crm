Pipeline & deal detail

Every frame works. Drag cards between stages (desktop) or use “Move stage” (mobile). Gated stages open “Complete to move”. Skipping a stage, missing KYC or a pending approval gives a blocked transition. Filters, Show US$, the template picker and the stage path all work too.

Stage rulesA lock on a column means it has entry requirements. Forward moves go one stage at a time; moving back is always allowed. Won is only reachable from Contract.
Complete to moveRequired fields, checklist, attachment and approval, with a progress count. If an approval is needed, the deal waits in its stage with a visible “Waiting for approval” band.
MoneyCards show full ₦; columns show compact total and weighted (× stage probability). US$ lines are optional and labelled ≈ with the rate. Deal lines use the locked FX rate.
Card signalsDays in stage (grey, then stale at 7d, rotting at 14d), close date (red if overdue), next step (amber if missing), last WhatsApp contact, owner.
