Home, by role

Five role-based Home layouts built from one widget grid. Each is shown at 1440px and 360px, light and dark. Everything works: date range, ₦/US$, lite mode, Customise (drag, resize, reorder, remove, add), AI actions, approvals, the programme picker, and live 24-hour and SLA timers.

Widget grid12 columns on desktop, widths S/M/L/XL (4/6/8/12). One column on mobile, with arrows instead of drag. Layout is saved per person.
Lite modeRings, bars and timelines turn into short numbers. Lists stay. Data refreshes every 60 s instead of live.
MoneyStored in ₦; switching to US$ is display only, and the rate and its time are shown. Compact (₦31.2M) in charts, full (₦12,400,000) in lists.
Honest AIEvery suggestion shows a “Why”, one action and “Not now”. Nothing runs without a tap.
