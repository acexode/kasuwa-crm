## What was hard / decided
- **Fail-open.** A screen hides a control only on an answer that says no. A failed or unread read draws the controls and the API's 403 stays the gate. That is also why no existing per-screen mock needed a `getMyCapabilities` answer.
- **`/scripts` is a client-side not-found, not an HTTP 404.** The read is made by the browser and a mock only reaches the browser. The spec asserts the not-found, not a status.
