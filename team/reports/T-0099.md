# T-0099 — Notifications contract

**What changed.** `docs/api/records.openapi.yaml` gains the notifications contract, all four operations
`x-kasuwa-implemented: false`: `listNotifications`, `getNotificationUnreadCount`, `updateNotification` and
`markAllNotificationsRead` (with `throughId`, D-L154). It also gains the `notifications` tag, the `NotificationCursor`
parameter, the nine `Notification*` schemas, and the `NotificationNotFound` and `NotificationsTooManyRequests`
responses (the latter with `Retry-After`). The `x-kasuwa-notification-kinds`,
`x-kasuwa-notification-display-fields` and `x-kasuwa-poll-interval-seconds: 30` extensions are in place.
`team/FEATURES.md` is regenerated. No existing example or schema was touched, and the 10 e2e mocks still load.

**What was hard.** The kind/objectType coupling is a `oneOf` of untyped constraint branches on a closed object, which
the suite's validator and sweeps accept. One regex needed the `throughId` field text reworded
("It carries only a notification id").

**Left out.** No implementation (T-0100) and no mocks (T-0101).

**Results.**
- records-contract-notifications 81/81, unmodified.
- All other platform contract suites and features-register pass.
- lint and typecheck pass with `--parallel=1`.
- The only failures in a full `tests/acceptance/platform` run were 3 tests in the two `*.pending.*` files, which are
  expected to fail.

**Open questions.** None. No dependency added.
