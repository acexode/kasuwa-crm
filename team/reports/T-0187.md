# T-0187 — Outbound send pipeline (depth)

## Summary

- **`sendMessage`** — `POST /api/v1/conversations/{conversationId}/messages` with closed body `{ body, purpose, idempotencyKey }`.
- Sends through **`createChannelAdapter`** (injected `CHANNEL` token) with session scope and contact-derived destination; idempotency key namespaced as `inbox:{conversationId}:{clientKey}`.
- Persists **`message`** (outbound, `body_redacted`) and **`message_delivery`** (0055) after a successful adapter send; updates `conversation.last_message_at`.
- OpenAPI: `sendMessage` marked `x-kasuwa-implemented: true`; new error codes `dnd_refused`, `provider_rejected`, `no_destination` on the admin envelope.
- **`AppModuleOptions.dndRegister`** optional override for acceptance (production remains fail-closed `unconfiguredDndRegister`).
- Acceptance: `tests/acceptance/inbox/pipelines.acceptance.test.ts` (outbound + typed errors; webhooks deferred).
