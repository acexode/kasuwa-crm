# T-0047 — Revoke DELETE on the cascade parents (Agent 1)

**State:** merged to `main` (#86), migration 0022.

**What changed:** `REVOKE DELETE ON "user", programme, business_unit, tenant FROM kasuwa_app`. A DO block asserts that kasuwa_app no longer holds DELETE or TRUNCATE on any of the four tables.

**Why:** a cascade from these tables bypasses RLS. On `main`, an A1-only agent deleted a business unit, which removed A2 with it, and a session with no programme deleted its whole tenant.

**Evidence:**
- Lead suite: 19/19 (10 of these failed on `main`).
- Neighbouring suites: 134/134.
- Integration: 109/109.

**Open:** a later re-grant of DELETE is caught by the suite, not by 0022 itself. The member-writable `status`/`email` lockout is T-0048.
