## What changed

**Migration `0044_notification.sql`.**
- Table `notification`: id, tenant_id, programme_id, recipient_user_id, kind, object_type, object_id, actor_user_id,
  source_event_id, created_at, read_at. Ids and codes only.
- RLS ENABLED and FORCED, in `TENANT_SCOPED_TABLES` and the `schema.ts` mirror. Every policy is `TO kasuwa_app`.
  - `notification_tenant` is permissive FOR ALL, tenant and programme, WITH CHECK identical to USING.
  - `notification_recipient_select` and `notification_recipient_update` are restrictive, on
    `recipient_user_id = app.current_user_id()`.
  - `notification_insert_as_actor` is restrictive: `actor_user_id = app.current_user_id() AND created_at = now() AND
    read_at IS NULL`. INSERT is not recipient-restricted.
- Grants: kasuwa_app gets SELECT, INSERT and `UPDATE (read_at)`, with no DELETE. The relay, auth, sweeper and PUBLIC hold
  nothing. kasuwa_test_admin gets full DML, as every table does.
- Keys:
  - Composite FKs to tenant, programme, recipient and actor, all ON DELETE CASCADE.
  - No FK on `object_id` or `source_event_id`.
  - CHECK `notification_kind_object_check`: the kind fixes the object type, and the vocabulary is the contract's three
    kinds (including `import_finished`/`import`).
- UNIQUE (source_event_id, recipient_user_id).
- Indexes, none a prefix of another: the recipient keyset, a partial unread index, programme and actor.
- A self-check at the end. No SECURITY DEFINER function (the census stays at two, asserted).

**Event registry (`@kasuwa/db` `outbox-events.ts`).** Added `account.owner_changed`, `deal.owner_changed`,
`activity.assigned` and `import.finished`. All are strict, ids only, and routed to `kasuwa.events.notifications`.
Re-exported from `@kasuwa/domain-core` as the same objects.

**Producers.** `appendOwnerChangedEvent` (domain-core) is called after the write in these places:
- contacts, accounts, deals and activities: create and update;
- merge and undo (D-L163): the survivor's owner on merge, and the restored contact's owner and the survivor's revert on
  undo;
- `import.finished`: one per job, from the worker's `endJob`, when a job finishes or fails.

Each producer takes the aggregate's row lock first (the UPDATE or INSERT it follows) and uses `randomUUID()` for the
event id. No event is appended for an unchanged owner, a clear (D-P23) or a self-assignment.

**Consumer.** `handleNotificationEvent(tx, event)` (domain-core) is registered in `apps/worker/src/main.ts` through
`outboxConsumerProcessor`, always on, behind no flag. It writes a notification only when all of these hold:
- the aggregate, re-read in the event's programme, is live (a merged-away contact is not) and its current owner equals
  the payload's `newOwnerId`;
- the owner is not the actor;
- the owner is an active member of the programme;
- the owner can read the object, judged on their own set: a deal needs a pipeline-reading set, and an import needs
  `contact.import`;
- `owner_user_id` is not withheld from the owner by field security. This needs `loadFieldAccessForUser` (see below).

It inserts with a plain INSERT in a SAVEPOINT, and treats 23505 on `notification_source_event_recipient_key` as already
delivered (D-L164, no ON CONFLICT, no RETURNING). Anything else rejects, so BullMQ retries. For `import.finished` the job
must be finished or failed, and the actor must be the creator.

**Reads and writes (`apps/api/src/notifications`).** Four routes:
- `listNotifications`: keyset, fetching `limit + 1` rows. The cursor is the notification id. A missing, foreign or
  deleted cursor row finds no position and gives an empty page with `nextCursor: null`.
- `getNotificationUnreadCount`: examines the 500 newest unread, counts the visible ones, caps at 99.
- `updateNotification`: the object's visibility is judged before any write, so a 404 changes nothing.
- `markAllNotificationsRead`: `kind` and `throughId` as the contract says. A `throughId` that is not the caller's own is
  one `400 invalid_value`, and the answer is the new visible count.

Names are resolved at read time under the reader's scope and field security (domain-core `notification.repository.ts`):
invisible objects are dropped, withheld names are null, and the actor is named only while a member of the object's
programme. A per-user, per-operation throttle (120, 120, 120 and 60 a minute) is decided before validation and sets an
integer `Retry-After`. The controller is in `RECORDS_CONTROLLERS`, so every answer is `no-store`.

**Contract and register.** The four operations are flipped to `x-kasuwa-implemented: true`. `team/FEATURES.md` is
regenerated (`pnpm features`; `features:check` was failing without it). New flag `crm_notifications` (see decisions).

## Decisions I made that the rulings did not cover

1. **Both user FKs are ON DELETE CASCADE.** The actor is cascade too: a notification without an actor cannot say who
   assigned it, and a user delete (D-L61, open) must not be blocked by inbox rows. 0043 used RESTRICT for its users.
2. **The INSERT policy also pins `created_at = now()` and `read_at IS NULL`.** Otherwise a row could be back-dated out of
   the newest-first window or inserted already read.
3. **New shared API: `loadFieldAccessForUser(tx, objectApiName, userId, programmeIds)` in `@kasuwa/metadata-engine`.**
   It is the same read as `loadFieldAccess`, under the caller's RLS, but for another user. N-R3 needs the recipient's
   field access, and the loader only took the session user. The doc comment says never to call it with an id from a
   request. This is a change to a shared package: please review it.
4. **`import.finished` is implemented, though the suite did not pin it.**
   - It is emitted for `finished` and `failed`, not `cancelled`: the user did that themselves.
   - It is emitted from the worker's `endJob`, so a header-stage failure notifies too.
   - Read-time visibility is `contact.import` in the programme (the import API's own rule).
   - The kind is exempt from the never-notify-the-actor rule, and requires actor = creator.
5. **No per-row events for an import that sets an owner.** The task says one notification per job.
6. **Merge and undo events.** Merge emits for the survivor if its owner changed. Undo emits for the restored contact (if
   its owner differs from the merged row's) and for the survivor's revert. Whether the restored contact deserves
   "assigned to you" is your call; it is one line to drop.
7. **Cursor = the raw notification uuid.** The contract says only that it carries a notification id.
8. **Feature flag `crm_notifications`.** It is presentation-only (the T-0101 bell and centre), default off, like
   `crm_search`. The producers, the consumer and the routes are **not** gated: an event appended and never consumed
   would sit in the queue, and the suite boots the worker and the API with no flag. Nothing reads the flag yet.
9. **Read rights.** Contacts, accounts and activities need only programme membership (RLS). Deals need
   `canReadPipelines`. A notification whose object lives in a different programme than the row is dropped.
10. **The throttle spends budget on 400s too**, as search does.
11. **Task file vs suite.** The task file says "100"; the suite and D-L157 say 500, and I built 500.

## Findings for the lead

- **D-L161's NDPA gate is still open.** The relay's jobs use `removeOnFail: false`, so a consumer failure parks the full
  envelope (tenant, actor, aggregate ids) in BullMQ's failed set indefinitely. The decision asked for the NDPA note
  "before T-0100 ships a consumer". I did not write it (governance is yours).
- **A new consumer failure mode:** `OutboxEventRefusedError` thrown by a producer (for example `programme_out_of_scope`)
  would now fail a user's write, since producers run inside the write's transaction. I believe every call site passes a
  programme from the session or the loaded row, but a producer bug is now a 500 on a write.
- **Slots.** My `node-slot-2` directory was removed by something while my shell pid was recorded in it, because a Bash
  tool shell dies between calls. I recreated it with a background `sleep` pid I own (42251) and killed it at the end.
- No dependency added.
