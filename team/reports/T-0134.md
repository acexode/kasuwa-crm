# T-0134 — P05 records to the design (contact and account)

Agent 11, branch `agent11/T-0134-p05-records`. Design: `docs/design/P05/Kasuwa Record Views.html` (20 frames).
Verification is visual only, per D-L201. Screenshots are in `.artifacts/parity/app/p05-*` (git-ignored). They were
taken against the live demo API, all D-L194 flags on, in Africa/Lagos time: 1440, 1024 and 360, light and dark,
plus the offline, loading and not-found states.

## What matches

- **Layout (P05/01, 03, 05–07, 10, 12, 13).**
  - 1440: three columns, 300 / fluid / 340, with 20px gaps.
  - 1024: fluid / 320, with the Insights | Details segmented switch heading the right column.
  - 360: underline tabs. The contact has Activity | Details | Insights; the account has Activity | Contacts |
    Details | Insights.
  - Every block renders once and the grid moves it, so the e2e hooks exist once at every width.
- **Header.**
  - Breadcrumb (hidden at 360, where the shell app bar has the back arrow).
  - 72/52px avatar: round for a contact; a 14px-radius accent tile with initials for an account.
  - 26/700 name with a badge: the contact's status, the account's tier.
  - Subtitle: company · account link · Owner. For the account: industry · LGA, State · "Part of" parent link · Owner.
  - 26px chips: since-year, DND, consent, language, preferred channel.
  - Follow button.
  - Action bar, laid out as the export does:
    - Call is the filled primary;
    - the rest are outlined at 14/600 with 20px glyphs;
    - four icon-only actions at 360, five labelled ones at 1024, everything at 1440;
    - the rest go in a More menu (`@kasuwa/ui` Menu).
  - "Log activity" opens a menu of Note / Task / Call. The `data-action="log-activity"` hooks are kept.
- **Phone app bar (T-0130 `appBar`).** "← Contact" / "← Account", search and a kebab (Edit, Note, Task, Call).
- **Details card.**
  - Collapsible sunken caps groups. Contact: Contact info, Personal, Consent, System. Account: Company, Location,
    System.
  - 12/600 labels, a mono phone, and a pencil on every field the edit sheet edits. Pencils carry
    `data-action="edit-contact"` / `"edit-account"`, so there is no separate Edit button.
  - System adds Created and Programme.
- **Timeline (`record-timeline.tsx`, shared with the deal).**
  - Day groups: Today, Yesterday, then the date.
  - 36px tinted type circles on a 2px connector; title plus "14:02 · actor".
  - Notes sit in the dashed accent box with "Not visible to customer".
  - Filter pills, with no counts.
  - A centred "Load older".
  - **The zone now reads WAT, not GMT+1.** `zoneName()` maps the African zones by their English long name
    (WAT/CAT/EAT/SAST/GMT). Chromium says "West Africa Time" and Node says "West Africa Standard Time"; both are
    mapped. "Last synced" uses the same name.
- **Account.**
  - Contacts as the export's tile grid: avatar, name link, status, mono phone, and WhatsApp / Consent ✓✗ chips.
  - Hierarchy card: the ancestors read up `parentAccountId`, root first and linked, then this account, highlighted.
    It uses the export's indents and elbows. The `account-hierarchy` hooks moved here from the old breadcrumb.
- **Right rail.**
  - Related: the contact's account, plus the flagged lists.
  - Consent history (contact): WhatsApp, promotional SMS/DND and marketing, from real data.
- **States.**
  - Offline: the inverse 48px band (`@kasuwa/ui` Banner), with the time in WAT.
  - Loading: the header with three chip pills; three columns at 1440, two at 1024, one at 360. The bar heights are
    the export's.
  - DENIED: the design's centred not-found styling (56px disc, 26px title, primary "Back to my contacts", the
    shared-link hint). It uses the generic `notFound.*` copy. It names no programme, owner or id, and has no lock and
    no Request access (CLAUDE.md 6.4, D-L195).

## What doesn't match yet, and why

- **No exact-total or fake data, by design.**
  - No "Customer since": the chip reads "Since {year}" from `createdAt`.
  - No VIP or "Key account" badge: the status or tier badge takes its place.
  - No Contact/Account ID: only UUIDs exist.
  - No job title, layout chip ("Contact · Sales v3"), recording, payments, invoices, WhatsApp bubbles, roll-ups,
    child accounts, buying roles or last-contact line.

  New cut spots are recorded in `team/reports/ui-audit/README.md` (D-L195).
- **Icons.** `notification_add` (Follow) and `star` are not in the subset. I used `notifications`, and dropped the
  Key-account chip. Adding them means `pnpm ui:fetch-fonts` and a re-pin; I left that to avoid a font-file conflict
  with the parallel builders.
- **Lead instruction I did not follow literally.** The lead asked for the record's name in the 360 app bar and to
  drop the h1. The export's 360 frames title the bar "Contact" / "Account" and keep the name as the h1 in the
  header card, so I followed the design. There is no duplicate, and the e2e `h1` hook is intact.
- **Day headings use the locale's date format.** In `en` that is "Sat, Oct 3"; the export, which is en-GB, shows
  "Mon 28 Sep".
- **Not run (D-L201):** unit, acceptance, integration, isolation and e2e.
  - Unit tests I knew would be affected are updated: the flag pin, and the account message-ids test now allows the
    flagged ids.
  - **Byte budgets are not measured.** The record pages now import `@kasuwa/ui` Button, Tabs, SegmentedControl,
    Menu and Banner, plus the new frame. The contact (249,000) and account (258,500) pins will probably need the
    end-of-programme re-pin.
