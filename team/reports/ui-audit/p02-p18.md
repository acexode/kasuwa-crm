# UI fidelity audit: P02 Admin Console + P18 Customisation Studio

Auditor group: p02-p18. Read-only. Design sources: `docs/design/P02/Kasuwa Admin Screen.dc.html`, `docs/design/P18/Kasuwa Studio Screen.dc.html` (exact values below come from inline styles there). The design has no 360 or dark frames, so I judged the app's 360 and dark shots against the v1.1 tokens (dark bg #151210, surface #1E1A17, etc.).

Code locations:
- Shell: `apps/web/components/admin/admin-shell.tsx`
- Screens: `apps/web/components/admin/{home,programmes,programme,roles,field-security,audit}-screen.tsx`, plus the tab files `agents-tab`, `sla-tab`, `dispositions-tab`, `scripts-tab`, `retention-tab`, `portal-tab`
- Shared pieces: `dialog.tsx`, `panel.tsx`
- P18 custom fields: `apps/web/components/admin/custom-fields/*`
- Type scale: `packages/ui/src/tokens.ts`

## P02 shared admin frame (top bar + settings menu) — every /admin route — Major gaps

These defects show on every P02 and P18 screen, so they are listed once here.

- [responsive][layout] **At 1024 the app keeps the menu as a fixed sidebar. The design makes it a drawer.** Design at 1024 (frames 02/04/06/10/26/34/40): hamburger in the top bar, content full width, and the menu opens as a 272px overlay drawer with scrim `rgba(20,16,12,.44)` and a shadow. The app switches at Tailwind `lg` = 1024px, so at exactly 1024 it shows the fixed sidebar and squeezes content to about 690px. That causes wrapped titles, cut-off tabs ("Portal u") and truncated filter selects. Fix: switch at `xl` (1280) instead of `lg` in `admin-shell.tsx`. This is shared.
- [responsive][layout] **At 360 the top bar wraps to two rows and covers the page.** The header is `h-16 flex-wrap`. Bell, EN, lite toggle and account icon drop to a second row that sits outside the 64px bar and on top of the eyebrow and breadcrumb. This happens on every screen ("BPO", "Users & Access", breadcrumbs, "Compliance" are hidden). Needs a single-row phone header: overflow menu, or hide language/lite in the account menu.
- [missing] **Top bar contents differ from the design.**
  - Design: hamburger (tablet only) · logo · "Kasuwa" · Admin chip · tenant switcher button ("apartment" icon + tenant name + expand_more, 36px high, bordered, 1440 only) · spacer · residency pill · help icon button · bell (borderless 40px, red 8px unread dot) · avatar (36px circle, accent-soft, initials).
  - App: no tenant switcher, no help, no avatar. Instead it has a bordered bell box, an "EN" language menu, a lite-mode toggle and a text "Sign out" link.
- [border] Top-bar icon buttons are bordered 44px boxes in the app. The design uses borderless 40px buttons with a `surface-sunken` hover.
- [copy] Residency pill: design "Data stored in: **South Africa (af-south-1)**", with the region in bold and the AWS region code shown. App: "Data stored in: South Africa", no bold, no code (`admin.shell.residency` and `getHomeSummary().dataRegion`).
- [spacing] Header height: design 60px, app 64px (`h-16`).
- [type] Logo word: design `<b>` 16px/700, app regular weight. The Admin chip is 22px high with 6px radius in the design; the app's is smaller.
- [spacing] Sidebar: design width 272px with padding `12px 10px 32px` and `gap 2px`. App is `w-72` = 288px with `p-3` and `gap-1`.
- [spacing] Nav rows: design `min-height 40px`, `padding 0 10px`, `gap 10px`, 20px icons. App uses `min-h-touch` (44px), `px-3`, `gap-3`, and "sm" icons that look about 16px. Sub-items are spaced about 37px in the design and 44px in the app, so the app menu is about 20% taller and pushes Integrations/Billing below the fold.
- [type][colour] Nav sub-item text: design 14px in `--k-text`. App uses `text-text-2`, so items look greyer and lighter (see "Clients", "SLA policies" and others).
- [state][colour] The active group header is not highlighted. In the design the open group that holds the current page ("BPO", "Users & Access", "Compliance") turns primary-text, with its icon in primary. In the app it stays plain text.
- [missing][icon] Design "Find a setting" has a leading search icon (20px, at left 10px, input `padding-left 38px`, height 40px). The app input has no icon and is 44px high with a larger 16px placeholder.
- [missing] Groups with no items (Automation, Integrations, Billing) show no chevron in the design. The app shows a chevron on all of them.
- [copy] Group label: design "Users & Access", app "Users and access" (`admin.nav.group.users`). Design also uses "DND & quiet hours" and "Agents & skills"; the app uses "and".
- [state] Attention dots on groups (Users & Access, Channels, Compliance) and the count badges on items (Users 2, MFA policy 2, Data-subject requests 1) do not show in any app shot. This may be because there is no badge data, but confirm `getSettingsMenuBadges` returns something in the demo.
- [layout] The sidebar does not reach the bottom of the page. It is `lg:sticky lg:max-h-screen`, so on pages taller than the viewport the white panel and its border stop (around y=830–960) and grey page background shows underneath (admin 1024, programme 1024, audit, FLS). In the design the nav is full height, `height:100%`, with `border-right`.
- [state] Programme tabs do not move the menu highlight. In the design the SLA, Dispositions, Scripts and Retention tabs highlight "SLA policies", "Dispositions", "Scripts" and "Retention" in the menu (frames 13/15/17/19). The app always passes `activeSettingId="programmes"` (`programme-screen.tsx:106`).
- [type] **Page title scale is wrong on every screen.** Design h1 is 28px/36px, weight 800, letter-spacing -.01em. App uses `text-h2` = 32px/40px, weight 700. Card and section titles: design 16px/700 (15px on programme cards, 18px for "Usage this month"). App uses `text-h5` = 18px/26px, weight 600. Intro paragraphs: design 15px with `max-width` ~720 on one line. App uses `text-body-md` 16px with `max-w-prose`/`max-w-2xl`, so it wraps to two lines. Either add a 28/800 page-title token or map these screens to the right sizes. This is shared.
- [colour] Primary actions that are not wired yet (New programme, Add role, New programme in the empty state, Import) render at `opacity-60`, which reads as a washed-out lavender button. The design shows solid primary. This was a deliberate choice, but it is a visible difference and should get a product decision.
- [dark] Dark mode follows the tokens (bg #151210, surface #1E1A17, primary-soft nav highlight). No token errors. The same structural issues apply.

## Shared/foundation issues seen
1. **Admin shell breakpoint**: the sidebar becomes a drawer at `lg` (1024) instead of above 1024. At 1024 every P02/P18 screen should use the drawer. One change in `admin-shell.tsx` fixes about 8 squeezed and overflowing layouts.
2. **Phone top bar wraps** (`flex-wrap` on `h-16`) and covers page headers on every admin screen at 360. The same component pattern probably affects other shells (LanguageMenu, LiteModeToggle, SignOutButton).
3. **Top-bar composition does not match the design**: no tenant switcher, help or avatar; it has bordered icon boxes, EN and lite toggles, and a text sign-out. The residency pill lacks the bold region and code.
4. **Type scale mismatch**: the designs use 28/800 page titles, 16/700 card titles and 14px body. The app maps to h2 32/700, h5 18/600 and body-md 16. A page-title/card-title token, or remapping, is needed across all admin screens.
5. **Control density**: the app uses `min-h-touch` (44px) everywhere, for nav rows, chips, tabs and inputs. The design uses 40px nav rows and inputs and 36px chips/tabs at desktop, so app pages are about 15–20% taller.
6. **Selected chip/tab pattern**: the design fills selected chips and tabs with primary and white text. The app uses primary-soft with a primary border (programmes filters, FLS object tabs, P18 object toggles).
7. **Search inputs lack the leading search icon** (nav, programmes, FLS, agents).
8. **Status/tag tone mapping**: the audit action badges all use one neutral style, and PII uses the warning tone instead of info.
9. **Unsaved-changes bar**: a full-width strip instead of the floating card, used in 3 places. **Dialog**: title size, footer divider and scrim differ. Both are shared components (`dialog.tsx` plus the inline bars). The bar should be extracted into one component.
10. **Sidebar height**: the sidebar does not fill a page taller than the viewport.
11. **Raw data shown to users**: lowercase object-type keys, UUID record labels, IANA zone names and long datetime formats. The design uses short "27 Sep, 14:32 WAT" formats.
