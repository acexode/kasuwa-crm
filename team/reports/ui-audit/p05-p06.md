## P05 Contact record (360°): `/contacts/[id]`. Verdict: **Major gaps**

Frames compared:
- P05/01 contact-1440-light -> p05-contact-1440-light
- P05/02 contact-1440-dark -> p05-contact-1440-dark
- P05/03, 04 contact-1024 light/dark -> p05-contact-1024-light/dark
- P05/05, 06, 07 contact-360 (Activity / Details / Insights tabs) -> p05-contact-360-light/dark
- P05/08, 09 WhatsApp window closed -> (no app equivalent)
- P05/14 FLS, 15 OFFLINE, 16 offline-360, 17/18 LOADING, 19 DENIED -> (none captured; judged from source `components/record/record-states.tsx`)

What the app has today: a two-column page (Details 1/3, Activity 2/3) plus a header card with Edit and Start script. The design is a three-column 360° workspace. Most of the design's content blocks are missing.

### Differences, worst first
- [layout] Column grid. Design desktop is `300px minmax(0,1fr) 340px` (Details rail / composer + timeline / insights rail). Design tablet is `minmax(0,1fr) 320px`, with an **Insights | Details** segmented switch heading the right column. App is `md:grid-cols-3` with Details taking 1 column and Activity 2, and it has no third rail at any width. Fix in `apps/web/components/contact-record/contact-record-screen.tsx:285` (screen-local; shared with account, see below).
- [missing] Header card content. Design has:
  - a VIP badge after the name;
  - the subtitle "Job title · Account link · Owner **name**";
  - a status chip row: Customer since, DND·promo SMS (danger), Marketing email ✓ (success), Marketing SMS ✗ (danger), language, Prefers WhatsApp, NIN verified;
  - a **Follow** outlined button at top-right;
  - a primary action bar: **Call** (filled primary), then WhatsApp, SMS, Email, Log activity, New deal, New ticket, Send payment link (outlined, icon + label). At 1024 the bar collapses to Call/WhatsApp/SMS/Email/Log activity/**More**.

  App shows only the avatar, the name, the programme name as subtitle ("Bank X Collections"), and Edit / Start script buttons. Design shows no Edit button: fields are edited inline with pencil icons.
- [missing] Breadcrumb. Design: "Contacts › Lagos Region › Name" (underlined links, chevron separators). App: "← Contacts" back link.
- [missing] Composer card above the timeline. It has channel tabs (WhatsApp / SMS / Email / Note, with an underline-active indicator), a "24-hour window open · 3h 11m 58s left" success pill with mono countdown, a textarea "Reply on WhatsApp…", attach / quick-reply / AI icons, the hint "Free reply inside the window", and a primary **Send** button. App has none. Its activity panel instead has outlined Note / Task / Call buttons.
- [missing] Timeline structure. Design:
  - the card title is "Timeline" with "All times WAT" on the right;
  - filter pills: All 8 (filled primary) and Calls/WhatsApp/SMS/Email/Notes/Tickets/Payments/E-invoices, each with a count;
  - day group headers (TODAY / YESTERDAY / MON 28 SEP, overline caps);
  - a vertical connector line between items, and coloured circular type icons (green WhatsApp, blue call, amber note);
  - rich item bodies: WhatsApp chat bubbles (green inbound, white outbound, timestamps), a call recording player with waveform and 1× speed, an "AI call summary" tinted panel, an internal note in a dashed amber box tagged "Not visible to customer", payment received (big mono amount + Paid badge), an e-invoice card (Validated badge + PDF button), an SMS bubble, an email card with attachment, and a resolved ticket row;
  - a "Load older activity" outlined button at the end.

  App: title "Activity", "Times shown in GMT+1", no filters, no day groups, no connector line. Every icon is the same lavender circle. Field-change audit rows ("Status changed … Not set → Qualified") make up most of the feed. The design timeline shows no field-change history rows. Fix in `components/record/record-timeline.tsx` (shared by contact, account and deal).
- [copy] Timezone label. Design is "All times WAT". App is "Times shown in GMT+1". Use the IANA zone's short name.
- [missing] Right rail, all of it:
  - **AI summary** card: What's happened bullets, Sentiment · last 8 contacts bar chart, Improving trend line, Open risks warning rows, "From 24 interactions. Check before acting." with thumbs up/down;
  - **Next best action** card (tinted primary-subtle) with Why:, a **Draft the message** primary button and a "Not now" link;
  - **Related** card with collapsible sections Deals / Tickets / Quotes / Invoices / Campaigns: count, rows with right-aligned mono amounts, status badges (Rotting 9d, Fresh, Open · 2h left, Resolved, Viewed, Paid, Part-paid, Overdue 9d, Clicked, Not sent) and "View all";
  - **Consent history** card: per-channel ✓/✗ rows with source and timestamp, plus an NDPA footnote.

  App has only a "Related" card with a single Account link in the left column.
- [missing] Left-rail structure. Design Details card:
  - a header chip "Contact · Sales v3" (layout name);
  - collapsible sections with sunken caps headers and chevrons: CONTACT INFO / PERSONAL / SYSTEM;
  - a pencil edit icon per editable field;
  - mono phone and IDs;
  - fields: Mobile, Work email, Job title, Preferred language, Preferred channel, Best time to call, Date of birth, Home phone, Contact ID (KSW-C-…), Source, Created, Programme.

  App: one flat Details list with no sections and no pencils. Status is a full-width native `<select>`, which the design does not have. Owner has an "Assign to me" link.
- [missing] **Identity** card: NIN verified panel (success-subtle, masked NIN in mono, check icon), Phone matches NIN record (Match badge), BVN (Not checked, dashed badge), and a privacy footnote. Not in app.
- [missing] **Addresses** card: "+" add icon, and Work · delivery (Primary badge) / Home blocks with State / LGA / Landmark rows. App shows State and LGA as plain detail rows.
- [missing state] WhatsApp window closed (P05/08, P05/09). Warning panel "24-hour window closed 2 days ago…", then approved-template radio cards (template name in mono, language · approval date, "Utility · Low cost" chip). Not built.
- [missing state] FLS (P05/14). Restricted fields show a lock icon + "No access" in place of the value: Date of birth, Home phone, NIN number, Home address, recording ("Recording · No access (restricted to QA and supervisors)" dashed box), invoice rows "Payment details restricted". In the app's field-change rows the redaction copy is "The value is not shown." The lock-icon field treatment could not be verified from screenshots. Check `data-locked` handling in `contact-record-screen.tsx:565` against this design.
- [state] Offline banner (P05/15, P05/16). Design is a full-width light band (dark mode: surface-raised) above the header card, with a cloud-off icon, **"You're offline."** followed by "2 changes queued — they'll sync when you reconnect. Showing what's saved on this device, last synced 09:12 WAT", a **View queue (2)** outlined button, a disabled Call button, Send relabelled **Queue**, and a "Queued" chip on edited fields. App `RecordOfflineBanner` uses `bg-inverse text-on-inverse` (a dark band in light mode) and has no queue count, no View queue button and no Queued chip. Fix in `components/record/record-states.tsx:18` (shared by P05 records).
- [state] Loading skeleton (P05/17, P05/18). Design: a header skeleton (64px circle, 2 lines, 3 chip pills), then **three** columns. Left has 5 × 44px bars; centre has 120px + 4 × 64px; right has 140px + 64px + 2 × 44px. App: header skeleton without chip pills, then **two** columns (5 and 4 rows, 40px bars). Fix the `skelCols` values in `record-states.tsx:30`.
- [missing state] Access denied (P05/19, P05/20). Design is a centred lock icon in a circle, "You can't open this record", the programme names, Record ID / Programme owner box, **Request access** primary + **Back to my contacts** outlined, and an audit footnote. App deliberately renders the generic 404 for out-of-scope ids (`app/contacts/[id]/page.tsx` comment). **This conflicts with CLAUDE.md guardrail 6.4** ("A record outside your scope is 404 … must not leak existence"): the design discloses the record ID, the other programme and its owner. This needs a decision (D-L) before it is built. Do not implement it as drawn.
- [responsive] 360. Design app bar is "← Contact" title + search + kebab (no logo, no EN/lite/sign-out icons). Below the header card are **three** tabs, Activity | Details | Insights, as an underline tab bar full-bleed. Header actions at 360 are icon-only square buttons (Call filled, WhatsApp, SMS, Email, More), and Follow is an icon-only button at top-right. App: generic top bar (K logo, search, bell, EN, lite, profile); a **two-option** segmented pill (Activity | Details); Edit / Start script buttons labelled. App activity buttons Note / Task / Call wrap onto 2 rows.
- [responsive] App field-change rows at 360 wrap "Name · Actor ·" onto two lines with a trailing orphan "·" separator (visible on every row). Design rows are single-line meta.
- [type] Design record name is ~28px bold with diacritics (Noto Sans). App is the same family but larger (~32px), with a subtitle line where the design has job/account/owner meta.
- [dark] Dark palette roughly matches tokens. Design dark CTAs, though, are a lighter indigo (`primary` dark, #5b5bd6-ish) with white text on Call/Send. In app dark the Contacts nav pill is a deep navy fill rather than the design's subtle tinted rail item. Note card in dark: design uses a dashed amber border on a dark surface; app uses a solid brown fill.
- [shell] The shell differs at every width; see the shared section. Design has a 72px icon rail with captions (Home, Inbox, Contacts, Accounts, Deals, Tickets, Reports; no Leads or Activities), a top bar with a wide search field "Search contacts, deals, tickets, numbers…" + `Ctrl K` kbd, a bell and an avatar. App has a 220px labelled sidebar (including Leads and Activities) and a top bar with a "Search" button, bell, EN locale menu, lite-mode icon and a "Sign out" text link.

Likely fix location: `apps/web/components/contact-record/contact-record-screen.tsx` (screen-local, roughly a rebuild of its layout), `components/record/record-timeline.tsx`, `components/record/record-states.tsx` (shared by records), `components/crm/crm-shell.tsx` (shared shell). New components are needed for the composer, AI summary, NBA, Related sections, Consent history, Identity and Addresses.
