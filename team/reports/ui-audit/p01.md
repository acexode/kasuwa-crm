## P01/1 Sign in — `/` — Major gaps

Frames compared: 01→p01-signin-1440-light, 02→p01-signin-1440-dark, 03→p01-signin-360-light, 04→p01-signin-360-dark (1024 app shots have no design frame; judged on tokens).

- [missing][copy] The app adds a **"Workspace" field plus helper text** ("The short name of your workspace, like sahel-bpo.") above Work email. The design has no workspace field: Work email comes straight after the divider. This adds about 112px and changes the form. It is a product decision (tenant slug), so escalate rather than delete: options are slug-from-email-domain, or a subdomain. `components/auth/sign-in-screen.tsx`.
- [missing] **"New to Kasuwa? Create an account"** is missing. It only renders when `signUpOpen` (the `self_serve_signup` flag) is on. The design always shows it. Decide whether the flag should hide it.
- [layout] "Sign in with SSO": the design is a **borderless text button** (transparent, `primary-text`, `domain` icon 20px). The app is `SECONDARY_BUTTON border-transparent`, so it draws a full-width white surface block (visible at 1440, 360 and dark).
- [icon] The Google and Microsoft buttons are missing the boxed **"G"/"M" glyph badges** (20×20, 1.5px currentColor border, radius 4, 11px/800). The app shows text only.
- [layout] Password reveal: in the design the eye button sits **inside** the input (input padding `0 4px 0 14px`, 44px icon button inside the border). In the app the eye sits **outside**, to the right of a shortened input.
- [layout] Panel width 520px vs app 1/3. At 1440 that is 480px; at 1024 it is 341px, which squeezes the headline into four lines, wraps the chips into 2×2 and wraps the trust lines.
- [type] Panel headline 44/52/800 vs app 32/40/700. Design breaks into three lines ("Every customer / conversation. / One place."); app breaks "conversation. One / place."
- [spacing] Channel chips are 36px tall in the design vs `min-h-touch` 44 in the app, so "Email" wraps to a second row at 1440. Design keeps all four on one row. They are non-interactive `li`s, so the 44px touch floor does not apply.
- [type] Logo mark 40px vs 32px; wordmark 20px vs `text-h4`, mark letter 22 vs 18.
- [type] H1 28/36 vs 24/32. Subtitle 15 vs 14.
- [spacing] Form column 400 vs 512. Controls 48 vs 44 tall. Stack gap 18 vs 16. Input font 16 and padding 14 vs 12.
- [layout] Vertical placement: design centres the column (`margin:auto 0`) with heading at y≈175. App is top-weighted with heading at y≈152. Design top-bar gap is 6px with buttons 44px high, which matches.
- [state] Checkbox: design is a custom 20px box, radius 4, primary fill with white check. App uses a native `accent-primary` checkbox, which differs in shape and focus ring.
- [responsive] At 360 the design top bar is **logo + icon-only lite toggle + globe "EN"**. The app shows no logo (the panel is hidden but `withPanel` is still true, so the top logo is suppressed below `lg`) and full "Lite mode" / "English" labels. `LiteModeToggle` / `LanguageMenu` support `compact`, but `auth-shell.tsx` hard-codes `compact={false}`.
- [missing][responsive] At 360 the design has a **compact trust footer** pinned at the bottom: border-top, 12px, "Encrypted connection · NDPA · POPIA · Data stored in Africa", 15px success-coloured icons (`authFooter`). The app has none, so nothing tells phone users about trust or residency.
- [dark] Dark colours match the tokens (indigo panel, near-black surface, lavender primary). The same structural gaps apply. The app's native checkbox in dark renders as a white square when unchecked (seen on TOTP 360 dark).

Fix location: `apps/web/components/auth/auth-shell.tsx` (shared by every P01 screen), `button-classes.ts` (shared), `sign-in-screen.tsx` (local).

## P01/2 TOTP — `/verify` — Major gaps (user-reported bug confirmed)

Frames: 21→p01-totp-1440-light, 23→p01-totp-360-light, 24→p01-totp-360-dark.

- [copy][state] **BUG, confirmed in the screenshot:** the alternative reads **"Text a code to"** with no number. Design: "Text a code to +234 803 *** 4567". Root cause, in `sign-in-screen.tsx` `submit`: the call is `router.push('/verify?challenge=' + challengeId)`. It drops `result.value.methods[].deliveryHint` (and `method` / `resendAvailableAt`), so `/verify` gets `hint=null` and `t('auth.mfa.alt.sms', { hint: '' })`. Fix options: pass `hint`, `method` and `resendAt` in the URL (the page already reads them), or have `/verify` fetch the challenge's methods server-side. Also hide the line when no SMS method or hint exists, rather than rendering the bare prefix.
- [layout] Other ways to verify: design is a **list of rows** (48px, 15px, leading icon in `text-2`, trailing `chevron_right`, bottom border on each row, left-aligned). App renders three **full-width bordered centred buttons** (`SECONDARY_BUTTON`).
- [type] Section label: design "OTHER WAYS TO VERIFY" is 12px/700, uppercase, 0.06em tracking, `text-muted`. App `text-h6` is sentence case and not uppercase.
- [layout] **No brand panel** (`showPanel={false}`). Design keeps the panel on all three MFA states.
- [responsive] **OTP cells overflow at 360.** App cells are 48px + 8px gap = 328px in a 320px column, so the sixth cell runs past the 20px gutter (visible in the 360 light and dark shots). Design cells are 44×52.
- [type] OTP cells: 44×52 mono 22/600 in the design vs 48×44 `font-mono text-h4` in the app. That makes them wider and shorter than the design.
- [state] Verify button: app is disabled at `opacity-60` (washed-out lavender) until all six digits are typed. The design shows the button at full colour. Consider keeping full colour and validating on submit, or confirm with design.
- [spacing] Icon disc 64 vs 56. H1 24 vs 28 (design wraps "Check your authenticator / app" at 400px; app fits on one line at 512px). Body 16 vs 15/23.
- [dark] At 360 dark, the native "Trust this device" checkbox renders bright white unchecked. Design uses a 20px box with a 2px `border-strong` outline on the surface colour.
- [responsive] At 360 the "Lite mode" label wraps to two lines and "English" is not shortened to "EN".

## Shared/foundation issues seen

1. **Control height 44 vs 48.** `button-classes.ts` (`PRIMARY/SECONDARY/FIELD`) uses `min-h-touch` (44). The design uses 48 for every input and primary/secondary button across P01 (and 15px for secondary labels). This needs a `control-lg` token or a height change. It recurs everywhere.
2. **H1 scale.** The design page title is 28/36 (32/40 on success). The app uses `text-h3` 24/32. There is no 28px step in `packages/ui/src/tokens.ts`; add one, or map to the design's display size.
3. **Column widths.** The app uses Tailwind `max-w-lg/xl/2xl` (512/576/672). The design uses 400/520/640 (stepper 880).
4. **The brand panel is dropped on most screens.** `showPanel={false}` on 1b, 1c, 1d, 2/2a/2b and 4, and P01/6 sits in the onboarding shell. The design shows the panel on **all** auth screens and on success. Panel width (520px fixed, not 1/3), headline (44/52/800) and chip height (36) are wrong in `auth-shell.tsx`.
5. **Phone layout of the shell.** The top bar should show the logo when the panel is hidden below `lg`, plus `compact` lite/language (icon + "EN"). The **trust footer strip** is missing. The 64px top bar is not implemented. "Lite mode" wraps at 360.
