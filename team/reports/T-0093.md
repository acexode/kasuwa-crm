   - I left the erased example out of getImport. The erased state is shown in `listImports` (`filename: null`, `rawFileDeletedAt` set), which satisfies R22's "one example shows an erased import".
   - A UI doing P28/A5 for an old job would want an erased getImport body to mock. The lead may want that check to skip `mapping` when `headers` is null, or may rule that the mapping is cleared with the headers.
2. i18n: the four new `Error.code` values have no `records.error.<code>` keys. This is contract only and the task did not list them; whoever draws T-0096 or T-0097 will need them.
