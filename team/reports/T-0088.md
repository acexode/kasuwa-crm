## What changed
Contract only. Every new operation, property and parameter is `x-kasuwa-implemented: false` until T-0089.

**`docs/api/records.openapi.yaml`** (version 0.13.0-T-0088)
- New schemas:
  - `CustomFieldKey` (`^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*__c$`, at most 63 characters). It is a helper, so the key rule is defined once.
  - The six encodings: `CustomFieldText`, `CustomFieldNumber`, `CustomFieldCurrency`, `CustomFieldDate`,
    `CustomFieldPicklist` and `CustomFieldBoolean`.
  - `CustomFieldValue` (the anyOf of the six), `CustomFields`, `CustomFieldsPatch` and `CustomFieldWithheldPath`.
- `CustomFieldNumber` is a single pattern:
  - a canonical decimal string with at most 18 significant digits;
  - no leading zero, trailing fractional zero, `+`, exponent or `-0`;
  - a value below 1 (`0.000…`) counts only its significant digits, so `0.0000000000000000001` is admitted.
- Where `custom` goes:
  - on Contact, in `ContactPendingProperties`;
  - on Account, in a new `AccountPendingProperties` allOf part (Account's own `properties` are unchanged);
  - on Activity and Deal, in their own `properties`.
  - Each one is an `allOf: [$ref]` wrapper carrying the flag and a description.
- `custom` on the four create bodies (`CustomFields`) and the four update bodies (`CustomFieldsPatch`).
- `withheldFields`:
  - Activity and Deal now take `anyOf: [the standard enum, CustomFieldWithheldPath]`;
  - Contact and Account items stay `string`, and their descriptions name the `custom.<key>` form.
- Filters: shared `CustomFilter` (`custom`, deepObject) and `CustomEmptyFilter` (`customEmpty`, a repeatable unique
  key list) parameters, on listContacts, listLeads, listAccounts and listDeals.
- Error examples:
  - `ValidationFailed`: `unknown_field`, `invalid_value`, `unknown_value`, `currency_mismatch`, `custom_too_large`,
    `field_withheld` and `filter_not_supported`;
  - `DealValidationFailed`: the subset that applies to deals;
  - `NotPermitted`, `AccountNotPermitted` and `DealNotPermitted`: a `field_not_editable` example at `custom.<key>`.
- Examples with `custom`:
  - createContact 201, createAccount 201 and createActivity 201 (new examples) and createDeal 201 (extended). Each
    shows a value and a withheld key;
  - a new listLeads 200 example;
  - a `custom` request example on each update. updateContact and updateAccount moved from `example` to named
    `examples`, and the original body is kept as the first one.
- A custom-field paragraph in the descriptions of the four create, four update and four list operations, and in
  `info`.

**`docs/api/admin.openapi.yaml`**
- The `custom-fields` tag and four operations:
  - `listCustomFields`: GET `/api/v1/admin/custom-fields?object=`, no permission and no 403;
  - `createCustomField`: POST;
  - `updateCustomField`: PATCH `…/{fieldId}`;
  - `retireCustomField`: POST `…/{fieldId}/retire`, with no body.
  - The three writes carry `x-kasuwa-permission: custom_field.write` and `x-kasuwa-audited: true`.
  - Every operation also publishes `501 NotImplemented`, because the admin mock answers an unhandled operation with its
    501 or throws.
- A new `FieldId` path parameter.
- Schemas: `CustomFieldObject`, `CustomFieldType`, `CustomFieldApiName`, `CustomFieldPicklistValue`,
  `CustomFieldPicklistConfig`, `CustomFieldCurrencyConfig`, `CustomFieldConfig`, `CustomFieldDefinition`,
  `CustomFieldLimits`, `CustomFieldList`, `CreateCustomFieldRequest` and `UpdateCustomFieldRequest`.
- Responses `CustomFieldValidationFailed` and `CustomFieldConflict`, and `limit_reached` in `Error.code`.

**Generated:** `tests/e2e/support/admin-mock.generated.ts` (45 operations) and `team/FEATURES.md` (4 rows, all
`not-started`).

**Permissions registry:** not touched. No contract or permission suite needs `custom_field.write` in
`packages/permissions` (`TENANT_ACTIONS`). Adding it is T-0089's job, together with the guard that uses it.

## What was hard
- **A merged suite contradicts R19.** `admin-ui/p02-live-api` ("keeps KPIs and skill cover on sample data") asserts
  that exactly `getProgrammeKpis` and `getProgrammeSkillCover` are not `x-kasuwa-implemented: true` in
  admin.openapi.yaml. R19 and D-L138 require the four custom-field operations to be `false`. Both cannot hold. That
  suite needs a lead edit: either `STILL_MOCKED` gains the four ids, or the check is limited to the P02 tags. I did not
  touch it. It fails 1 of 12 on this branch, as above.
- **The deal-timeline validator ignores `pattern`.** Its `activityProblems` checker supports `oneOf` but not `pattern`.
  With `oneOf: [enum, CustomFieldWithheldPath]`, a standard name such as `dealId` matched both branches, and the
  self-check "the Activity checker demands dealId present or withheld" failed. I switched Activity and Deal
  `withheldFields.items` to `anyOf`, which the lead's edit allows.
  - The cost: the e2e mocks' hand-written validators do not evaluate `anyOf`, so they no longer check those two
    `withheldFields` item enums.
  - The acceptance validators still do (the custom-fields suite rejects `nonsense`, `custom` and `custom.tier`).
- **The R8 echo check is a substring test** over every error body, so example values had to avoid words that occur in
  error text. Examples:
  - a picklist value must not be `large` (in `custom_too_large`) or `value` (in `invalid_value`);
  - a label must not be `Type` or `Config`.
  - The values used (`gold`, `silver`, `platinum`, `retail`, `referral` and so on) were chosen with that in mind.

## Open questions
1. `p02-live-api` against R19: a lead edit is needed (see above).
2. **updateCustomField on a retired field.** No ruling covers this. I wrote `404 not_found` (the one out-of-scope body),
   but the field is still visible in `listCustomFields`, so `404` sits oddly with "404 means you cannot reach it". The
   alternative is a `400` detail such as `field_retired`. Please rule before T-0089.
3. **Adding a picklist value that already exists:** I wrote `400 duplicate_value` (the create code). The suite pins it
   only at `config.values`; the update path I described is `addValues`, and the code at that path is not pinned.
4. **`CustomFieldText` and line breaks:** the suite leaves this to the builder. The pattern refuses only NUL, so line
   breaks are admitted. If `text` is meant to be single-line, given that `textarea` is a refused type, it needs one
   more rule.
5. **Contact and Account `withheldFields` items** stay `type: string`, as the suite expects. Tightening them to
   `anyOf: [standard names, CustomFieldWithheldPath]` would mean listing their standard names, which no ruling asked
   for.

## Risks I am aware of
- Tenancy: the contract keeps definitions tenant-wide and not programme-scoped (ADR-0008). There is no
  `programmeId` in any admin body, and every out-of-scope case is the one 404 body.
- Field security: withheld keys are omitted and listed as `custom.<key>`. A filter on a key withheld in any reachable
  programme is `400 field_withheld`, and no custom sort exists. T-0089 must enforce all of this in SQL (ADR-0016 item
  6), not only in the serializer.
- No error example echoes a value. T-0089 has to replace Zod's default messages to keep that true on the wire.

## Security review fixes (D-L140)
I merged `origin/lead/T-0088-acceptance` first (a00d22f: the leads mock withholds `custom.<key>` inside `row.custom`).

Applied:
1. **The size limit no longer measures withheld bytes.**
   - The API limit is 16 KiB, measured on the merged `custom` restricted to the keys the caller can read in the row's
     programme plus the keys being written. Withheld and retired values never count.
   - The 32 KiB DB CHECK is described as a backstop only.
   - This is stated on CustomFields and CustomFieldsPatch, in the four update and four create descriptions, and in
     the `customTooLarge` example summary.
2. **A key that fails the key pattern** is `unknown_field` at `custom` (or at `customEmpty` for that filter) and is
   never echoed. It is documented on CustomFields, CustomFieldsPatch, both filters and the write descriptions. A
   `customMalformedKey` example is added to ValidationFailed and DealValidationFailed.
3. **`CustomFieldNumber`** has `maxLength: 40`.
4. **At most 500 definitions per object, retired included.** Beyond that is `409 limit_reached` /
   `field_limit_reached`, stated on createCustomField. The limit of 100 active fields stands.
5. **Check order on the three admin writes:** `401`, then `403` (`custom_field.write`), then `404`, then `400`
   (`field_retired` and the body codes), then `409`. A repeat retire answers `200` only after the 403 and 404 checks.
6. **Logging:** custom values are never logged or traced, whatever the classification (D-P25). This is stated on
   CustomFields, in the record write descriptions and on createCustomField.

**Not applied, because the change would break a pin in custom-fields-contract:**
- **CustomFieldLimits data.** The test "CustomFieldLimits: closed, fieldsPerObject 100, picklistValues 500,
  bytesPerRecord 32768" pins:
  - the exact key set (`expect(keysOf(limits)).toEqual(['bytesPerRecord', 'fieldsPerObject', 'picklistValues'])`);
  - the required set;
  - `bytesPerRecord` const `32768`.

  So neither `apiBytes: 16384` nor a renamed `storageBytes` can be added, and neither can a definitions cap.
  `bytesPerRecord` stays 32768. Its description now calls it the 32 KiB database backstop and says the 16 KiB API limit
  and the 500-definition cap are not yet published as data.
- **`includeRetired` on listCustomFields.** The test "listCustomFields takes exactly one parameter: object, required, a
  CustomFieldObject" pins the parameter list to `['query object true']`. listCustomFields therefore still lists
  retired fields, as R12 says.

Both need a lead edit to the suite before they can land.
