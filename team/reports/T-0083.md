## Escalation first: the two `@p07-budget` journeys cannot pass as written

Both `@p07-budget` tests in `tests/e2e/p07-activities.spec.ts` (lines 1998 and 2022) call
`page.clock.setFixedTime(new Date(NOW))` and then `measurePageLoad()`. `measurePageLoad` waits for
`performance.getEntriesByName('first-contentful-paint')`. Playwright 1.63's fake clock replaces
`window.performance` with a fake whose `getEntries`, `getEntriesByName` and `getEntriesByType` return `[]`
(`fakePerformance()` in `playwright-core/lib/coreBundle.js`). So the wait times out after 30 s on every run,
whatever the screen does. The page itself rendered (P07 was in the failure snapshot).

Three acceptance assertions fail because of this, and nothing else fails:
- "runs every @p07 journey, and every one passes" (36 of 38 pass; the two budget journeys time out);
- "proves /activities inside its byte budget on the production build (@p07-budget)";
- "pins /activities at or under 255,000 bytes and within 5% of what this run measured" (no
  `p07-budget.json` is written, because the test dies before writing it).

I did not edit the spec, which is pinned by sha256. A fix the lead could make: drop `setFixedTime` from the
two budget tests (the screen needs no fixed clock to load), or call it after `measurePageLoad`. Both change
the pinned hash, so the lead has to re-pin.

### Behaviour worth knowing

- **Nothing is optimistic, and a form closes after the re-read.** After a 200, the screen re-reads the buckets
  (and the calendar, once opened) before the form closes or the row's controls come back. Without this, a
  form reopened at once could be seeded from the pre-write row. A move judged against stale times would then
  pass locally and be refused by the server.
- **The version written** is the higher of the row's and any version a write answered with (`versionOf`).
- **A 409 in a form** shows `activities.reschedule.stale`, re-reads, and rebases: an untouched field takes the
  fresh value and is never sent; a field both people changed keeps the typed value with
  `record.conflict.otherChange` under it (`data-conflict`). If the item is gone after the re-read, the form
  says so and Save is off.
- **A 409 on a quick action** (complete or a snooze preset) shows the generic stale message and re-reads. The
  person presses again.
- **A row offers no control for a field it withholds.** No complete when `completedAt` is withheld, and no
  snooze when `dueAt` is (D-L84's exception in the suite header). Otherwise write controls show to every
  caller and the 403 is the gate.
- **The calendar never places an item without its times.** An event that began before the window sits on the
  first day shown. One whose time is withheld (the planted case) is not drawn at all, which the spec allows.

## What was hard

- **The fixed clock and FCP.** See the escalation.
- **The zone.** It is read after mount, so the server render never guesses it and hydration matches.
- **Time formats.** `hour: '2-digit'` renders "09:00 PM", which the spec's clock oracle (rightly) does not
  read as 9 PM. Times use `hour: 'numeric'`.
- **Windows-shell grep.** `run-e2e.mjs` spawns Playwright with `shell: true` on Windows, so a `|` in
  `--grep` becomes a cmd pipe (EPIPE). I used a pipe-free pattern for the other screens' run.
