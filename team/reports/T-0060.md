## What changed

### The build (b798f28)

**`packages/db/migrations/0027_activity.sql`** is the only migration.

- **Order of work.** Locks are taken up front in a fixed order. Then the D-P24 clash pre-check, which counts only custom objects named exactly `activity`, plus catalogue-named custom fields on a standard `activity` object. Then `contact` gains `UNIQUE (tenant_id, programme_id, id)` (D-L77).
- **Columns.** Exactly the 15 the suite pins.
- **CHECKs:**
  - `type IN (note, task, call)`, NOT NULL;
  - at least one target;
  - `due_at` and `completed_at` on tasks only;
  - `version >= 1`.
- **Foreign keys.** Six composite FKs, each carrying `tenant_id`; the contact and account FKs go through `(tenant_id, programme_id, …)`.
  - Target and user FKs: RESTRICT on update and delete (D-L76).
  - Tenant and programme FKs: CASCADE on delete, RESTRICT on update.
- **Row-level security.** ENABLED and FORCED. `activity_isolation` has WITH CHECK identical to USING. A restrictive `activity_no_delete` policy stops `kasuwa_app` deleting anything (ratified as a D-L77 amendment).
- **Grants.** No TRUNCATE for `kasuwa_app` or any role it can enter.
- **Triggers.** 0018's `updated_at` trigger and 0019's `version` trigger are attached.
- **`app.guard_activity_keys_fixed()`** fixes `id`, `tenant_id` and `programme_id`. It is an allow-list: it raises 42501 unless both `current_user` and `session_user` are `kasuwa_migrator`. It is SECURITY INVOKER with a pinned `search_path`, and EXECUTE is revoked.
- **Metadata.** A standard `activity` object and eight field_defs (`subject` and `body` are pii), backfilled for every tenant, with a proof step.
- **Self-check.** The migration ends by asserting its own security posture.

**Other files:**
- `schema.ts` mirror, plus `activity` in `TENANT_SCOPED_TABLES`.
- The catalogue entry (`StandardFieldType` gains `textarea` and `datetime`).
- One seeded activity per tenant, on the seeded accounts.
- `packages/db/src/activity-guards.integration.test.ts`.
- Two unit tests that pinned a two-object catalogue.

## Risks I am aware of

- **Tenancy.**
  - This is a new tenant-scoped table, protected by the composite keys, the policy, the column grants and the key guard.
  - The suite's isolation cases pass: visibility per programme, cross-programme and cross-tenant writes and retargets refused, and no existence oracle.
- **PII.**
  - `subject` and `body` are pii, and nothing here logs them.
  - T-0061 must never log or return a Postgres `detail` for activity errors.
- **Hot-table lock.** The contact unique key holds ACCESS EXCLUSIVE on `contact` (and the catalogue) for about 4 to 12 s per million rows. That is acceptable only while no cell is populated (D-L65, D-L77). The header gives the split for the first populated cell.
