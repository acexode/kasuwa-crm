## What changed

- Migration `0047_product_catalogue.sql`: five programme-scoped tables with composite FKs, RLS ENABLE+FORCE, full DML grants to `kasuwa_app`, seed-friendly CASCADE, partial uniques on price-book entries, and `touch_updated_at` triggers.
