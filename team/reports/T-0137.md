## What matches the design

- **B (1440):** the page column (1116 + 32px padding), the 26/800 title with the count subtitle, the 40px
  Mark all read and Preferences buttons, the 240px filter card (Show: All, Unread, Mentions, Approvals at
  40px/14/600 with primary-soft active; Type: 36px/13/500 rows with sunken active), and the bordered list
  card of day groups with "n unread" per complete day.
- **Rows:** 40px tinted type tile (36 in the bell), the sentence as a 15/21 title (bold unread, 500 read),
  the object line (15px glyph, noun, name), the time at the inline end, and the 10px dot (primary, or a
  border-strong ring when read) in a 44px target. No row tint for unread.
- **B (1024):** pills over the card (36px, solid primary when chosen). **E2 (360):** the shell's app bar
  (`appBar` with the count as subtitle), Mark all read text button and tune button, a sideways-scrolling
  pill row, a full-bleed list, and the **Alerts tab** on the bottom bar (Home, Inbox, Deals, Alerts).
- **B2:** 56px sunken bell circle, 17/700 title, body, "Set preferences", disabled Mark all read (disabled
  tokens, never opacity).
- **A, A3:** 440px panel under the top bar at the inline end, radius 14, shadow-3; header with count,
  Mark all read (text) and gear; 30px pills; day groups; footer "Open notification centre →". No close
  X (Escape, outside press, the bell). A3: 44px sunken done_all circle, "You're all caught up", "Show all"
  under the Unread pill.
- **D:** `/settings/notifications`: breadcrumb, title, matrix card (event column + five 88px channel
  columns, WORK / MARKETING AND DATA / FINANCE / SECURITY), Quiet hours and Email digest cards.
- **C, A2:** `NotificationToast`, `NotificationToastStack` and `CriticalBanner` in `@kasuwa/ui/components`,
  drawn to the export and shown on `/design/components-2`.
- Times now use the 24-hour clock ("10:15").

## What does not match, and why

- **Tablet Type select** is drawn as pills: T-0101 I20 requires the kinds visible at 1024.
- **"Tasks" type entry** added after "Assignments": the API filters one kind at a time.
- **No body line and no Approve/Reject/Open/Reply** on rows: notifications store no text (D-P29), and the
  three served kinds are assignment and import rows, which the design draws without actions.
- **Phone header:** the shell always draws its 56px app bar (it carries the bell that I20 needs at 360), so
  the design's large "Notifications" title is the app bar's title, and Mark all read and tune sit in a row
  under it.
- **Toasts and banner are not mounted on any screen:** nothing raises them yet (no push, no sign-in alerts,
  and the list is never read on a timer, I5). I removed the two flags I had first added for them, since they
  would gate nothing.
- **Alerts tab badge** not drawn: the count store lives in the bell's chunk, and putting it in the shell
  would cost every page.
- Bell dropdown uses its own modal panel, not the T-0129 `Popover`: that one is non-modal, and I8/N13
  require a focus trap and an inert page.
