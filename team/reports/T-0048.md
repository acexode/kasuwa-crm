# T-0048 — Guard UPDATE on "user" (Agent 1)

**State:** merged to `main` (#87), migration 0023. It adds a trigger, `app.guard_user_update()` (SECURITY INVOKER), firing as `zz_user_update_guard` before every UPDATE on "user".

**What changed:**
- No app-role caller may change any email.
- Only an active tenant admin may change another user.
- Anyone else may change their own row, but not its status or role.
- It is a trigger rather than a policy, so the MFA row lock keeps working.
- It closes the T-0047 status lockout.

**Evidence:**
- Lead suite: 22/22, of which 11 failed on main beforehand.
- Neighbouring suites: 172/172. Integration: 109/109.
- Migration review: pass, with every bypass refused on real Postgres 16. Its fixes are applied: both identities are guarded, the admin must be active, and the trigger fires last.

**Open:**
- The forged `app.user_id` residual (ADR-0010).
- Stopping the last admin from deactivating themselves is app-level and not built yet.
- INSERT on "user" is covered by D-P16 / T-0049.
