## What changed

Four commits on top of the suite commit:

- `feat(flags)`: `crm_record_view`, default off and not production-ready, with a unit test.
  `team/FEATURES.md` regenerated (one new flag row).
- `feat(i18n)`: 55 ids under `record.*` in all eight full catalogues: field labels for the
  details panel and the timeline, language names, activity types, timeline copy, the activity
  form, the edit sheet and the record's refusals. Drafted by me for fr, sw, ha, yo, ig, pcm and
  ar, so they are Beta under D-P1. Four French passthroughs are declared with reasons
  (`Igbo`, `Yoruba`, `Swahili`, `Note`). `pnpm i18n:validate`: every menu language is
  structurally sound; none fell off the menu.
- `feat(web)`: the screen. It replaces the `/contacts/[id]` scaffolding (`record-view.tsx` is
  deleted).
  - `app/contacts/[id]/page.tsx` calls `notFound()` unless both `crm_record_view` and
    `crm_records` are on. It also calls it for an id that is not a uuid. It passes a
    server-resolved message bundle to the client island.
  - `lib/contact-record-api.ts`: getContact (with `owner` and `account`), updateContact,
    listContactTimeline, createActivity, updateActivity (complete only), getCurrentUser and
    listMyProgrammes. Every path is relative and every answer is Zod-parsed. No function takes a
    scope argument. Unit-tested in `contact-record-api.test.ts`.
  - `components/contact-record/`:
    - `record-model.ts` (pure, unit-tested): the detail rows, value labels, phone format,
      times, merging timeline pages, the edit draft and `changedFields`, and error copy.
    - `use-contact-record.ts`: state. Contact and timeline are read in parallel and fail
      independently. Nothing is optimistic. A 409 re-reads the record. A logged activity
      re-reads the newest timeline page and lays it over the top.
    - `contact-record-screen.tsx`: shell, header, details, related account, the
      loading/not-found/unavailable/offline states, and an Activity/Details toggle on mobile
      (P05/02).
    - `record-timeline.tsx`, `activity-form.tsx`, `contact-sheet.tsx` (the full edit, in
      `admin/dialog.tsx`), and `message-ids.ts` (no `'use client'`).
  - `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`: route row and a `crm_record_view` paragraph.
- `test(testing)`: `run-e2e.mjs` puts `crm_record_view` on the existing `crm_records` process
  and publishes `webRecordViewUrl` naming it. `support/stack.ts` declares the key. No fifth Next
  process is added. Because of this, the `@harness` tenancy spec now drives the real P05 screen
  against the real API. The ready state carries `data-testid="record"`; the not-found and
  unavailable states keep their scaffolding hooks.

## What was hard

- The tenancy spec and the new screen share one route. Putting the flag on the records process
  meant `data-testid="record"` could appear only in the ready state. Otherwise the tenancy
  spec's "can see own record" check would pass while the page was still loading.
- `en-XA` and language names. `Intl.DisplayNames` is not pseudo-localised, and endonyms are
  not served data, so either would fail the pseudo check. The record therefore names languages
  through `record.language.*`, and an unknown tag is drawn as record text.

## What I left out, and why

- Completing a task seen only on the timeline. The contract gap is D-L85 / T-0080: the screen
  draws no complete control for a task it holds no served version for.
- Reassigning an activity's owner and reopening a task. D-L81 allows reassigning; there is no
  design frame for it in this slice.
- No link from the account summary. T-0063 has not served `/accounts/[id]`.
- Everything D-L69 hides (composer, AI summary, recordings, NIN, addresses, Follow) and
  P05/05's offline queue (D-P21).
- The D-L84 colour-lint widening. Not in this task's scope.
