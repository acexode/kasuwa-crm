## What was hard

- **Drop geometry.** The spec's drag drops at `column.top + min(height/2, 120)`. With the page
  scrolling vertically, a tall target column's top sat above the viewport or under the sticky header,
  so the drop hit nothing. The fix: each column is at most a screen tall (`max-h-screen`) and scrolls
  its own cards. Because a card dragged out of a scrolling column would be clipped, the drag moves a
  fixed-position, inert copy that has no `data-*` hooks.
- **Escape after a refusal.** The confirm button had focus, became disabled while sending, and
  Chrome dropped focus to `<body>`. The panel's own key handler then never saw Escape. Dialogs now
  close on Escape from a document listener.
- **Stale-write races.** A drop sends the version from the freshest copy of the card (looked up at
  drop time, not at pointer-down). The prompt's confirm stays disabled until the rebase has applied
  the re-read deal, so a quick second click can never send a version that was already refused.

  builder).

  list view is desktop only (R13), and its switch is hidden below `md`.
