## What changed
- **`admin_live_api`** (default OFF, `productionReady: false`): the P02 programme pages read the real
  admin API. KPIs and skill cover (no endpoint; deferred with telephony and WFM) render labelled sample
  data (`data-source="mock"`, translated "Sample data").
- **D-P1:** `BETA_LOCALES` and `MENU_LOCALES` are derived in `@kasuwa/i18n`; `SUPPORTED_LOCALES`
  stays attested-only. The menu shows a translated Beta badge and a help link to a server-only
  `/translations` page. Fixing an axe `nested-interactive` violation, the menu options are now the
  controls themselves (arrow-key movement).
- **D-P11:** `region.law.body` is retired; `region.law.guidance` loses the "choose Nigeria" clause;
  `region.law.sales` carries the adopted sentence.
- **D-L43:** fonts are self-hosted only. **D-L44:** a 3,448-byte currency subset (₦, ₵); no route
  loads `noto-sans-latin-ext` on its critical path.
- Fixes found against the real API: unconfigured retention shows "no rules saved yet" and the first
  save sends version 0; archive sends the exact programme name.
