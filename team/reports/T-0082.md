## What was hard

- **Cursors with a moving key.** The id-only cursor must resolve a row that may have moved, been completed or become
  invisible since. Validity and position are read in the same transaction, and the position never leaves Postgres.
- **"Today" in arbitrary zones.** The DST days and zones that skip midnight are why there is a confirm step and a
  bisection fallback, not just "add 24 h".
- **ICU accepts more than IANA.** `new Intl.DateTimeFormat(..., { timeZone: 'EAT' })` resolves to Africa/Nairobi,
  and `EST` to America/Panama. The validator needs the canonical list plus a region-link rule.

   suites (a lead acceptance edit).
2. **A withheld time and the span rule.** With `startsAt` withheld and `endsAt` editable, the caller can learn the
   start from `invalid_range`/`too_long` answers by moving the end (the suite pins that moving `endsAt` succeeds in
   that state, so refusing the write outright is not open to me). It is a narrow oracle on a field the caller cannot
   read. A ruling would close it: either refuse a time write when the other time is withheld, or accept the oracle.
3. **D-P18 is still OPEN** in DECISIONS.md. I implemented the rights the suite pins (D-L81 and D-L87: `activity.write`
   moves anyone's task or meeting in the programme, `qa_analyst`/`client_portal` 403), with no new permission.
