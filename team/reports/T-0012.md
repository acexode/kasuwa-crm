## What changed
- **Migration 0017.** Replaces the one-factor-per-type unique constraint with two partial unique indexes,
  one for the confirmed factor and one for the pending factor. Adds `user_mfa_backup_code` (tenant_id,
  composite FK, RLS ENABLED and FORCED, WITH CHECK = USING). The app may UPDATE only `used_at`. Also added
  to `TENANT_SCOPED_TABLES` and to the reset drop list.
- **Backup codes** (`packages/auth-crypto/src/backup-codes.ts`). Ten codes of about 49 bits each, stored as
  HMAC-SHA256 over (tenant, user, code) under a wrapped per-set data key. ADR-0011 explains why there is
  no argon2id here.
- **Pre-session enrolment** (`/auth/mfa/enrol`, `/enrol/confirm`) is bound to the sign-in challenge and
  allowed only when the user has no factor. Backup codes work as a sign-in factor. **Signed-in routes**
  (`/auth/me/mfa*`) let a user replace a factor or regenerate codes. Every write requires the current
  factor.
- **Failure counters survive the request transaction.** `CommitThenFailInterceptor` raises the error only
  after the counters have committed.
- **Review fixes:**
  - Per-user `FOR UPDATE` serialisation of factor writes.
  - The challenge-path confirm only promotes the pending factor and never deletes one.
  - `no-store` on the token responses from `/auth/sign-in` and `/auth/mfa`.
