## What changed

- **`packages/ui/src/components/`**, all new files, exported from `part-2.ts`. `index.ts` re-exports that
  barrel, so everything is in `@kasuwa/ui/components`.
  - `table.tsx`: Table, TableHead/Body/Row/HeaderCell/Cell, TableCellTitle, TableSelectHeader/Cell (44px
    column, 18px box), TableBulkBar plus BulkAction, TableFooter plus TableFooterRange.
    - Header: sticky, 40px, sunken, 12/700 caps at .04em in text-2, column dividers, `shadow-table-header`.
    - Rows: 52px comfortable or 36px dense; a selected row is primary-soft.
    - Footer: 56px, mono "Showing", 30px mono rows-per-page radio group, prev/next pager.
    - Numeric cells sit in `<bdi dir="ltr">`, so phone numbers and signed timers keep their order in RTL.
  - `card.tsx`: Card (radius 12, bordered, padding lg/md/sm/none, optional 18/700 title, meta and actions).
  - `menu.tsx`: MenuPanel, Menu (WAI-ARIA menu button), MenuItem (radio items with the leading check;
    primary/danger action rows; mono meta), MenuSection (overline), MenuSeparator, Popover (non-modal
    dialog).
    - Panel: radius 10, padding 6 (4 when `tight`), shadow-2.
    - Rows: 40px, radius 6, selected primary-soft.
  - `dialog.tsx`: Dialog, ConfirmDialog, ConfirmPanel, OverlayCloseButton.
    - Dialog: radius 14, 440 or 640, 85vh, fixed 60px header and footer; the divider and shadow react to
      scroll.
    - On a phone the Dialog is full screen: 48px close first, 17/700 title, a single 52px full-width
      primary.
    - ConfirmDialog: 360px, padding 22, a 40px tinted icon disc, alertdialog, focus on the safe action.
  - `drawer.tsx`: Drawer (400px, max 90vw, from the inline end; it becomes the bottom sheet below 640px) and
    BottomSheet (radius 20 top corners, grab handle, 56px header, 48px close, 48px shared-width footer
    buttons, max 90% height).
  - `overlay.ts` and `portal.tsx`: shared overlay behaviour.
    - Esc closes. Focus goes to the first field, or to `initialFocus`.
    - Tab is trapped. Siblings of the portal are `inert`, and the page is scroll-locked.
    - Focus returns to the trigger. Scroll edges are tracked.
    - `preview` draws an overlay in place for the gallery, with no portal, trap or aria-modal.
  - `toast.tsx`: Toast, plus ToastProvider/useToast.
    - Look: success on inverse with an underlined action; error and info on surface-raised; warning on
      warning-soft.
    - Placement: bottom-centre on a phone, bottom-right on a desktop.
    - Timing: 6s auto-hide, paused on hover and focus. Errors stay.
    - Live regions: role=status for most toasts, role=alert for errors. Both regions are always mounted.
  - `stepper.tsx`: 26px circles (done in success with a check, active in primary, todo outlined), 24×2
    connectors. Below 640px it becomes an optional "Step 3 of 5 · Clean up" line with a progress bar.
  - `empty-state.tsx`: two layouts.
    - `start` (Patterns): 48 disc, 26 glyph, overline, 17/24 title, 14/21 body, action, footnote.
    - `center` (Components): 64 disc, 32 glyph, 18/700 title, 280px body.
    - Tones: primary, neutral, info, danger.
  - `skeleton.tsx`: Skeleton (line, line-sm, circle, block) and SkeletonGroup.
    - SkeletonGroup draws one 1.4s shimmer sweep, hidden under reduced motion via `data-k-shimmer`.
    - After 8s it shows the caller's "Still loading on a slow connection…" in a polite region.
  - `banner.tsx`: offline (inverse, with an outlined action), info (with an optional spinner), success,
    warning, danger (role=alert).

## What doesn't match yet, or was left out

- **Contact column resize handle and inline cell edit.** The v1.1 table shows both. They are behaviours,
  not part of the static spec, so they are not built.
- **Numbered pager.** v1.1 Components draws numbered pages, which D-P13 forbids (no totals). The footer is
  P04's prev/next with a page label.
- **Secure-payment banner** (primary, with a white 16% disc) is not built. Its disc colour is an rgba
  literal with no token; it needs a token decision when telephony needs the banner.
- **Select-style list.** The trailing check with primary-text 600 is T-0128's Select. MenuItem draws the
  P04 saved-views style: a leading check.
- **Sheet swipe-down to dismiss** and the "Discard changes?" guard are not built. Esc, close and a scrim
  press all dismiss.
- **The Drawer has one footer** for both its desktop and phone forms. A ghost Cancel suits the desktop
  drawer, but the phone sheet draws a bordered Cancel. The caller chooses.
- **Spec differences inside T-0128 components** that my parts use:
  - The confirm dialog's buttons are 0 12 padded, where the design uses 0 16.
  - Specimen fields stay 44px on desktop in the gallery's phone sheet frame.
