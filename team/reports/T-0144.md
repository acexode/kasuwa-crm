## Zone naming (lead ruling: "WAT" in every locale)

- **New helper.** `apps/web/lib/zone-name.ts` exports `zoneAbbreviation(locale, at, timeZone?)`:
  1. It maps the English long name to WAT, WAST, CAT, EAT, SAST or GMT, which covers engines that differ
     on the long name.
  2. Otherwise it uses the first real name among the en-NG, en-KE, en-ZA and en-GH short names.
  3. Otherwise it falls back to the locale's own offset.
  4. An unknown IANA id throws `RangeError`, as `Intl` does.
- **Callers.** All of these now use the helper:
  - `record-core.zoneName`
  - `admin-format.formatZone`
  - `home-format.zoneName`
  - `retry-time.formatRetryTime`, which still falls back to "UTC" for an unknown zone
  - the onboarding zone step in `simple-steps.tsx`
- **Removed.** `retry-time.zoneNameLocale`, and the duplicate `AFRICAN_ZONES` and `OFFSET_ONLY` copies.
- **Effect.** A French audit header and lockout line now say "WAT" where they said "UTC+1".
- **Test.** `apps/web/lib/zone-name.test.ts` covers:
  - WAT, EAT, SAST and CAT in seven locales
  - the offset fallback (Kolkata in French)
  - the throw
  - the audit, lockout and record paths agreeing
- **Not changed.** `activities/activity-format.ts` already names zones with en-NG in every locale
  (`zoneParts`). Its `zoneLabel` export has no callers.
