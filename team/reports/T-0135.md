## What does not match yet, and why

- **Shell and Next dev badge.** The page padding is 24px against the design's 28px; that comes from the T-0130 shell.
- **Kept beyond the design, for existing behaviour and test hooks.**
  - The search field in the filter card.
  - The Sort select above the list.
  - All stages, Edit deal and Assign to me on the deal.
  - The Requirements and Checks cards.
  - The won cards under the won box: the e2e asserts that won cards are visible.
- **Not used from T-0129, on purpose.**
  - `Dialog`/`BottomSheet` for Complete to move and the move sheet. They have no `data-testid`, subtitle, footer note,
    560px size, or lock against Escape while a move is in flight. Those are e2e hooks and D-L102 behaviour.
  - `Card` for the deal cards. Its padding is 24 and its title 18; the export draws 16 and 16.
  - `Menu` for the pipeline dropdown. The export itself is a native `<select>`, which is the better picker on the target
    phones.

  These are worth a T-0129 follow-up (a `testId` prop, a subtitle, a busy lock) rather than forking the look.
- **Icons substituted, to avoid a font-binary conflict with the parallel builders.** These names are not in the subset:
  - `table_rows` is drawn as `list`;
  - `local_shipping` as `inventory_2`;
  - `hourglass_bottom` (rotting) as `hourglass_top` in danger.

  The real names should go in one consolidated icon pass.
- **Dates.** `en` writes "Oct 31" where the design (en-GB) writes "31 Oct". That is the locale, not this screen.
- **Timezone label.** The shared timeline says "GMT+1"; the design says "WAT". That is `record-core`'s `zoneName`, shared
  with P05.
- **Not built (no data or contract).**
  - The Commit and Renewal badges, the deal number, and Forecast, Territory, Lead source and Type.
  - The FX rate-lock banner, the price book, and product lines.
  - The "Request KYC check" action.
  - Approvals inside the prompt, and the attachment upload.
  - All four rows are in the ui-audit README's cut table.
